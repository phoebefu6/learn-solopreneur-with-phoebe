The six sessions 🧭 · from selling hours to running a business

The running case is Marlow, a constructed solo data consultant, two years out of an analytics team, selling dashboards and clean-ups at $110 an hour. One anchor client sends most of the work; small clients send the rest; Marlow can work about 45 hours a week. Marlow and every Marlow number are invented for teaching and modelled, and every page says so. Illustrative, not financial or tax advice.
